Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

The developer of the MoneyApp application ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

MoneyApp operates on a self-hosted server model.

  • What we store: Financial transactions, categories you create, configured budget limits, and photos of scanned receipts.
  • Where we store: All data is transmitted via network protocol and saved in a standard database on the developer's private server (hard drive).

2. Camera Usage and Third-Party AI Services

The application requires camera access for the optical character recognition (OCR) feature. When scanning a receipt, the image is transmitted to the servers of standard public artificial intelligence services (e.g., Gemini). Image processing is governed by the data privacy policies of these providers, who may use the transmitted receipts to train and develop their neural network models.

3. Notifications

The application uses exclusively local push notifications (reminders to log expenses). Notifications are generated by your phone's operating system based on a timer. External mailing servers are not used.

4. No Analytics or Data Sharing

The application is completely free of advertising integrations (Ad SDKs), marketing trackers, and hidden telemetry systems. The application developer does not share or sell your information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Your financial data is stored on the server exactly as long as you actively use the application. If an account shows no activity for 24 months, all associated data may be automatically and permanently deleted from the database.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your data.
  • Correct inaccuracies in transactions.
  • Complete data deletion: You can request the permanent deletion of your account, transaction history, and receipt photos from our server.

To initiate the procedure for completely erasing your digital footprint, send a request to the email address specified in the "Contacts" section. The request will be fulfilled within 3 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If such data is identified in our database, it will be deleted immediately.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will become effective upon posting on this page.
